# Curbside Chef

I'm the owner of a fast food business and I want to create an app that helps me with the register. Shows the menu with a picture of the dish. A POS system.

Key requirements:
- Works seamlessly on a tablet and on PC/desktop with a modern, simplified, touch-friendly layout and an appealing warm culinary color palette.
- One shared register interface.
- Trilingual support: English, French, and Arabic with full Right-to-Left (RTL) layout switching when Arabic is selected.
- Customer bill printing formatted for standard thermal receipts (80mm) immediately after entering an order, plus kitchen tickets formatted in French.
- Quick cash calculation (presets, change owed) and card payment checkout.
- Menu management section (protected by manager PIN) where I can add items with photos, update prices, remove items, or mark items as out of stock.
- Dine-in and Takeaway order tagging with sequential order numbers (#001, #002...).
